Replyora

The visual and verbal identity for Replyora — the AI customer-conversation platform. Use this as the single source of truth for every post, graphic, and asset.

Logo

Two primary lockups. The circular badge is the hero mark (profile picture, stamps, avatars). The horizontal wordmark is for headers, footers, and wide spaces. Note the signature open dot “°” after the wordmark — it represents a live conversation and should always be kept.

Clear space & sizing

  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the “o” in replyora.
  • Minimum size: badge no smaller than 40px; wordmark stays legible above ~120px wide.
  • Always keep the open dot “°” — it’s part of the mark.

Don’ts

  • Don’t recolour the logo outside the palette.
  • Don’t stretch, rotate, or add drop shadows.
  • Don’t place the burgundy wordmark on a busy photo — use the cream badge instead.

Colour palette

Burgundy + warm oat is the signature. Lead with oxblood and oat; use rose and blush sparingly as accents. This warm, editorial palette is what makes Replyora look premium — protect it.

Brand voice

Premium, modern, and helpful — never gimmicky or pushy. We teach and demonstrate. We sound like a sharp, calm expert who happens to have built brilliant tech.

Confident, not loud Clear & concise Helpful first Premium Human Quietly bold

Do

  • Lead with the customer’s pain, then the fix.
  • Use real numbers and short, punchy lines.
  • Show the product working; let proof do the selling.
  • Stay warm and plain-spoken.

Don’t

  • Don’t use hype words (“revolutionary”, “game-changer”).
  • Don’t hard-sell or stack exclamation marks.
  • Don’t sound like a generic chatbot.
  • Don’t over-explain — cut every spare word.

Visual style

Layout

  • Generous whitespace — let the oat background breathe.
  • One idea per graphic; centre-set serif headlines.
  • Tracked uppercase eyebrow above each headline.
  • Logo discreetly in a corner, never crowded.

Imagery

  • Warm, minimal flat-lays (cream paper, soft shadows).
  • Real chat screenshots styled in brand colours.
  • Founder shots with warm, natural light.
  • Avoid cold, blue, “techy” stock imagery.
