# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 August 2026
**Last updated:** 7 August 2026

Replyora ("**Replyora**", "**we**", "**us**", "**our**") is a social media management platform that helps businesses and agencies plan, create, schedule and publish content to social networks, review it with their clients, and see how it performs. Replyora also offers an optional AI website chatbox add-on ("**Service**").

> **Pre-launch status.** Replyora is currently pre-launch and is operated by its founder, based in Sydney, Australia. A registered operating entity and ABN have not yet been established and will be added here at official launch. Until then, this policy applies to any early/trial use of the Service on that basis.

We handle personal information in accordance with the **Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)** and the **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)**, and, where applicable, the **EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**. We also comply with the **Meta Platform Terms** and **Developer Policies** and, where you connect TikTok, the **TikTok Developer Terms**.

By using the Service you agree to this policy.

## 1. Who this policy covers and our role

- **Account holders** (agencies, studios and businesses who sign up). For your account, billing and usage data, Replyora is the **data controller**.
- **Connected social accounts and content.** When you connect a client's (or your own) Instagram, Facebook or TikTok account and manage their content, that data belongs to the account owner. For this data Replyora acts as a **processor / service provider on behalf of you and your client** (the controller). You are responsible for having the client's authorisation to connect and manage their accounts.
- **Website visitors** who interact with a Replyora-powered chatbox on a business's website (if the chatbox add-on is used).

## 2. Information we collect

**a. Account information you give us**
- Identity and contact details: name, email, business/agency name, phone.
- Your clients' brand details you add: brand name, logo, colours, pillars, brief notes, package details.
- Content you create or upload: captions, post ideas, images and videos, calendars, and approvals.
- Account settings and support communications.

**b. Payment information**
- Billing is processed by **Stripe**. We do **not** store full card numbers. We receive limited billing details (e.g. name, plan, last 4 digits, subscription status) to manage your subscription and any add-ons.

**c. Social platform data (Meta — Instagram & Facebook, and TikTok)**

When you connect an Instagram, Facebook or TikTok account, you authorise us — through the platform's official login — to access and use the following on your and your client's behalf:

- **Access tokens** issued by the platform so we can act on the connected account (stored securely; see section 10).
- **Account/profile information:** the account's ID, username/handle, name, profile picture, follower/following and media counts, and (for Facebook) the Pages you manage and their linked Instagram Business/Creator accounts.
- **Content:** existing posts/media and captions we display in the visual grid, and the posts, reels, carousels and stories you create and publish or schedule through Replyora.
- **Insights/metrics:** reach, impressions, engagement, saves, follower growth and similar performance metrics we use to build your Reports.

We request only the permissions needed for the features you use (for Meta, these include reading basic profile and media, publishing content you create, reading insights, and — for Facebook — listing and managing the Pages you authorise).

**d. Chatbox add-on data (processed on behalf of the business)**
- Chat messages between website visitors and a business's chatbox, and any lead details a visitor provides (e.g. name, email, phone, enquiry).

**e. Information we collect automatically**
- Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, actions taken, and diagnostics.
- Cookies and similar technologies (see section 8).

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information. You should not configure the Service to collect sensitive information without a lawful basis and appropriate consent.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:
- Provide and operate the Service — display your connected accounts and their live grid, generate captions and post ideas, and let you plan, schedule and **publish content to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok on your behalf**.
- Produce **performance reports** from the metrics the platforms return.
- Run the optional website chatbox, capturing and routing leads for the relevant business.
- Process payments and manage subscriptions and add-ons.
- Provide support, secure and improve the Service, and send service communications (and, with consent where required, marketing you can opt out of anytime).
- Comply with legal obligations and platform policies, and enforce our Terms.

**How we handle Meta Platform Data specifically:** we use Instagram and Facebook data **only** to provide the features you request (showing your grid, publishing/scheduling your content, and your analytics). We do **not** sell it, we do **not** use it for advertising or to build advertising profiles, and we do **not** share it except with the sub-processors in section 5 needed to run the Service. Our use complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

## 4. AI processing

To generate captions, post ideas and chatbox replies, relevant content you provide is sent to our AI providers (**Anthropic** and/or **Google (Gemini)**) via their APIs. We configure these services so that **your content is not used to train their models**, consistent with the providers' API terms. AI output may be imperfect; see our Terms.

## 5. Who we share information with (sub-processors)

We share information only as needed to run the Service, with vetted providers under confidentiality and data-protection obligations:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Neon | Primary database (Postgres) |
| Cloudflare R2 | Media/asset storage |
| Netlify | Application hosting and delivery |
| Meta Platforms | Instagram & Facebook connections, publishing and insights |
| TikTok | TikTok connection and publishing |
| Anthropic / Google (Gemini) | AI caption, content and chatbox generation |

We may also disclose information to comply with the law or valid legal requests, to protect our rights or users, and in connection with a business sale (with continued protection of your data).

## 6. Storage and international transfers

Personal information may be stored and processed on servers located outside Australia (for example, in the United States or the EU) by our providers. Where we transfer data overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with the APPs and, for GDPR data, use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, then delete or de-identify it within a reasonable period, unless a longer period is required by law (e.g. financial records). Social access tokens are deleted when you disconnect an account or delete your workspace. You can delete clients, posts, assets and connections at any time.

## 8. Cookies and tracking

We use essential cookies to run the Service (e.g. authentication) and, with your consent where required, analytics cookies to understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may break parts of the Service.

## 9. Disconnecting accounts & deleting your data

You are in control of the social accounts you connect and the data we hold:

- **Disconnect a social account at any time.** In Replyora, open the client → **Integrations** and choose **Disconnect** for Instagram, Facebook or TikTok. This immediately stops our access, **revokes and deletes the stored access token**, and removes the connection.
- **Revoke from the platform directly.** You can also remove Replyora's access from your **Facebook/Instagram settings → Apps and Websites**, or your TikTok app settings.
- **Delete specific data** (clients, posts, assets, invoices) from within the dashboard at any time.
- **Delete your whole account and all associated data.** Go to **Settings → Data**, or email us (below). We will delete your workspace and the personal information we hold about you — including any Instagram/Facebook/TikTok data and tokens — within **30 days**, except where we must retain limited records to meet legal obligations.

**To request deletion or ask a data question, email hello.replyora@gmail.com** with the subject "Data deletion" and the email address on your account. We will verify your identity and confirm when it's done.

## 10. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encryption in transit and at rest, strict per-workspace tenant isolation, access controls, and least-privilege handling of secrets and social access tokens. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify affected users and regulators of eligible data breaches as required by the **Notifiable Data Breaches** scheme.

## 11. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent or opt out of marketing. Account holders can exercise many of these directly in the dashboard, or by emailing **hello.replyora@gmail.com**. Website visitors who used a business's chatbox should contact that business (the controller); we will assist.

## 12. Children

The Service is not directed to individuals under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new "Last updated" date and, for material changes, provide additional notice.
