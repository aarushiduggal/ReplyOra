# Replyora — Brand Kit

**Socials, simplified.**

A calm, editorial, minimal identity: a bold black wordmark, a porcelain base, and two whisper-soft pastels. The logo does the talking; colour stays quiet.

## Logo

The wordmark is **replyora.** — "reply" in a high-contrast *italic serif*, running tight into "ora." in a **heavy sans**, finished with a bold full stop. It always reads as one word; never split "reply" and "ora." or add space between them.

- **Companion mark** (for small sizes — favicon, app icon, social avatar, watermark): the heavy **o** or the bold **.** on a porcelain or ink tile. Use it whenever the full wordmark would fall below ~32px or needs to sit in a square.
- **Clear space:** keep breathing room equal to the height of the "o" on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** ~120px wide on screen; ~30mm in print. Below that, switch to the companion mark.
- **Do:** black wordmark on porcelain, blush, sky or white; reverse to porcelain on ink for dark sections.
- **Don't:** recolour the wordmark, add shadows/outlines/gradients, stretch or condense it, or set a pastel as the type colour.

## Colour

The neutrals carry the brand; the pastels are soft section washes, never text.

- **Ink** — `#1A1A1A` — the logo, headlines, body text
- **Stone** — `#8C877E` — secondary text, captions, hairlines
- **Porcelain** — `#F3F0EB` — primary page background
- **Soft blush** — `#F0E3E2` — section wash / accent one
- **Soft sky** — `#E3EBF0` — section wash / accent two
- **White** — `#FFFFFF` — cards and panels on porcelain

**Balance:** roughly 70% porcelain and white, 20% ink type, 10% blush and sky washes; Stone only for muted detail. Alternate blush and sky down the page for gentle rhythm. Keep pastels as backgrounds — because they're so light, they must never be used as text colour.

## Typography

- **Display serif** — the italic "reply", pull-quotes and editorial moments. Canva font: **Playfair Display** (Italic). *(Swap for the exact serif you used in the logo if different.)*
- **Heavy sans** — the "ora.", eyebrows, buttons and labels. Canva font: **Archivo Black** *(or Anton)*.
- **Body & UI** — paragraphs and interface text. Canva font: **Archivo** *(or Outfit / Poppins)*, Regular and Medium.

**Hierarchy:** eyebrow = heavy sans, uppercase, letter-spaced, small; H1 = heavy sans or italic serif; body = body sans at 16px, generous line height; captions = body sans in Stone.

## Voice

Calm, confident, plain-spoken, a touch editorial. Short sentences. Comfortable in lowercase. Talk about "socials," not "social media presence." Sell the feeling of things handled, not the feature list. Anchor line everywhere: **"Socials, simplified."**

## Imagery

Soft editorial photography with lots of porcelain space, pastel washes and black type laid over. Warm, real, aspirational-but-attainable — the aesthetic of a well-run small brand. Avoid stocky corporate stock photos and busy collages.

## Set it up in Canva (Brand Hub)

1. **Brand Hub → Brand Kits → (your kit) → Colours** — add each hex above and name them (Ink, Stone, Porcelain, Soft blush, Soft sky, White).
2. **Fonts** — set brand fonts: Heading = Playfair Display, Subheading = Archivo Black, Body = Archivo.
3. **Logos** — upload the wordmark in dark and reversed, plus the companion mark, so they're one click away in any design.
