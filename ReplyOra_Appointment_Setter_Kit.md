# ReplyOra — Appointment Setter Kit

Everything to hire cold callers who book **appointments** (15-min demos) onto your calendar.
They cold-call/DM your agency leads → book the appointment → **you** run the demo and close.

---

## 1. How you pay them (commission)

Keep it commission-only, and pay on **quality**, not just activity:

| Trigger | Pay |
|---|---|
| A **qualified appointment that shows up** | **$20** (per appointment) |
| That lead **converts to a paying client** | **+$100 bonus** |
| Client **cancels within 30 days** | bonus clawed back (not paid) |

- Pay **weekly** against the tracker (they invoice you; pay offshore via Wise/PayPal).
- Start with **1–2 setters**, run 2 weeks, keep the ones who book real ones.
- Sanity check: even at $120 all-in to land a $200/mo Agency client that stays ~12 months
  (~$2,400 LTV), that's an excellent cost to acquire.

### What counts as a "qualified appointment" (say this up front)
All five must be true, or it doesn't get paid:
1. A real **social media agency / freelance social media manager** (managing ≥1 client).
2. The person on the call is a **decision-maker** (owner / founder / lead).
3. They agreed to a **specific date + time** (not "maybe email me").
4. It's booked on the **Calendly** link (auto-logged).
5. They **showed up** (or rescheduled once and then showed).

## 3. The cold-call script (booking the appointment)

**Goal of the call = book the meeting. Nothing else.** Don't try to sell the product — sell the meeting.

**Opener (permission-based pattern interrupt):**
> "Hi, is this [Name]? … Hey [Name], I'll be honest — this is a cold call. Can I have 20 seconds and
> then you can tell me to get lost?" *(smile, pause — most say "go on")*

**Hook (their world, not ours):**
> "I work with **ReplyOra** — we give social media agencies one place to run all their clients:
> plan the grid, get client approvals, send branded reports and invoices, schedule Instagram + TikTok.
> Quick question — right now, how are you juggling content and client approvals across your clients?"
> *(let them answer — this qualifies them)*

**The ask (book it):**
> "Cool — that's exactly what we fix. The founder's doing free 15-minute setups this week for a few
> agencies — he'll actually set up one of your clients in it live so you can see it, no charge.
> Are you better **Thursday morning or Friday afternoon**?"

*(Always offer two time options, never "are you free?" — assume the meeting.)*

**Confirm + lock:**
> "Perfect — I'll send the calendar invite to [email] now. It's 15 mins with [Founder]. Can you do
> [day] at [time]? … Done. Keep an eye out for the invite 🙌"

**Objection handling:**

- **"I'm not interested."** → "Totally fair — most agencies I call already have *a* tool. The reason
  they still take the 15 mins is the client-approval + invoicing side, which the schedulers don't do.
  Worth a look — Thursday or Friday?"
- **"We already use Later/Planoly."** → "Perfect, keep it — this sits on top for the client stuff
  (approvals, branded reports, invoices). Founder will show you in 15 mins. Thursday or Friday?"
- **"Just send me info."** → "Happy to — but honestly the 15-min live setup lands way better than a PDF,
  and it's free. I'll send info *and* pencil a time you can cancel anytime — Thursday or Friday?"
- **"No time right now."** → "No worries, that's why it's only 15 and on your calendar. Morning or
  arvo usually easier — which suits?"
- **"How much is it?"** → "$200/mo for up to 10 client brands — about $20 a client, and you can mark
  it up to clients. The founder will walk you through it — Thursday or Friday?"

**Voicemail (leave one, then DM/text):**
> "Hi [Name], it's [You] — I help social media agencies run all their clients in one place, approvals
> and invoicing included. Doing free 15-min setups this week. Shoot me a text back on this number or
> I'll try you tomorrow. Cheers!"

**Text / DM follow-up (send right after):**
> "Hi [Name] — [You] here, just left you a voicemail 🙂 We give agencies one workspace for all their
> clients (approvals, branded reports + invoicing, IG/TikTok). Free 15-min live setup this week —
> want me to grab you Thursday or Friday?"

## 5. Onboard a new setter (day one, ~30 min)

1. Send them: the **lead list**, this script, the **Calendly** link, and the **tracker** (their tab).
2. Give them their **ref code** (e.g. `REF-MIA`) — they put it on every booking so pay is attributed.
3. Do **3 practice calls** with them role-playing you as the agency (test tone + objections).
4. Rule: they log **every** call in the tracker (dial → outcome → booking). Paid = qualified + showed.
5. Review after day 1, then daily for the first week.

---

## 6. Two things to decide before they dial

- **Phone numbers:** cold *calling* needs numbers — your lead sheet has IG + websites, not phones.
  Either enrich the list with phone numbers, have setters grab each from the agency's site/Google
  before dialing, or have them book via **DM/email** instead of calls.
- **Calendar:** set up a **Calendly** with a 15-min "ReplyOra demo" event so bookings drop straight
  onto your calendar and into the tracker.
